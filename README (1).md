## Project Overview

This project is **Scenario 1** of a Smart City analytics capstone, completed as part of the
PhysicsWallah Data Analytics with AI program (NSDC & PwC certified, 2026).

Acting as a data analyst at a consulting firm, I was tasked by a city Mayor to benchmark her
city against peer cities of similar size and recommend where to invest next — presented in a
way a non-technical stakeholder can understand at a glance.

- **Dataset:** 5,000 cities × 15 urban performance metrics
- **City Benchmarked:** City2720 (Population: 319,006 | Medium Tier)
- **Peer Group:** 2,951 cities of similar population size

---

## Key Findings

| Metric | City2720 | Peer Average | Rank | Verdict |
|--------|----------|-------------|------|---------|
| Smart Infrastructure Score | 29.7 | 42.7 | 2,174 / 2,951 | Below Average |
| Energy Consumption (lower=better) | 756 | 547 | 2,761 / 2,951 | 38% Above Avg |
| Public Transport Usage | 43.2% | 44.3% | 1,509 / 2,951 | At Median |

**Top 3 Investment Recommendations:**
1. **Energy Modernisation** — consumption 38% above peer average; smart grid investment can cut it 30%+
2. **Smart Infrastructure Upgrade** — 26th percentile; target reaching peer median within 3 years
3. **Bus Rapid Transit (BRT)** — near median now; top peers reach 87–95% adoption
